# Terminology Standards

## Pacific Insurance PH

Pacific Insurance PH is the insurance agency using the system.

The platform is being built for Pacific Insurance PH.

Clients, applications, tasks, relationships, and workflows belong to Pacific Insurance PH.

## Pacific Cross

Pacific Cross is an external insurance provider.

Pacific Cross supplies:

- Insurance products
- Underwriting
- Policy issuance
- Claims adjudication
- Commissions

Pacific Cross does not own the system.

## Client

A Client is a customer of Pacific Insurance PH.

The client may hold one or more Pacific Cross policies.

## Policy

A Policy is an insurance contract issued by Pacific Cross and serviced by Pacific Insurance PH.

## External Contact

An External Contact refers to a Pacific Cross representative or other third-party contact.

Examples:

- Territory Sales Manager
- Claims Officer
- Commission Officer
- Travel Insurance Officer
- Vendor

## System Naming Convention

All system documents, workflows, dashboards, and database records should be written from the perspective of Pacific Insurance PH.

Examples:

✓ Pacific Insurance PH Client

✓ Pacific Insurance PH Application

✓ Pacific Insurance PH Renewal Workflow

✓ Pacific Insurance PH Claims Workflow

✓ Pacific Insurance PH Relationship Program

✗ Pacific Cross Client

✗ Pacific Cross CRM

✗ Pacific Cross Workflow
