# Dashboard & KPI Framework

## Pacific Insurance PH Agency Operations Platform

# Purpose

Define the dashboards, metrics, and key performance indicators available to Pacific Insurance PH users.

The dashboard serves as the operational command center of the platform.

Its purpose is to:

- Highlight urgent work
- Monitor business performance
- Prevent missed opportunities
- Improve visibility across applications, renewals, claims, and travel insurance

# Dashboard Design Principles

## Principle 1

Actionable Information First

The dashboard should prioritize:

- What needs attention
- What is overdue
- What requires follow-up

before showing reports and analytics.

## Principle 2

Operational Visibility

The dashboard should help staff understand:

- Current workload
- Bottlenecks
- Client needs

## Principle 3

Business Visibility

The dashboard should help management understand:

- Revenue opportunities
- Renewal performance
- Growth trends

# Success Criteria

The Dashboard Framework is successful when:

- Matt can understand agency performance within 30 seconds
- Eman can understand today's workload within 30 seconds
- Urgent issues are immediately visible
- Revenue opportunities are not missed
- Client follow-ups are proactively managed
- Operational bottlenecks are identified quickly
